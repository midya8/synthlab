Initial observations are counted separately. They filter the complete grammar class using only their labels. The policy cannot use other target labels. Future answers are simulated from supplied function tables, not obtained from a live oracle. Exact, noiseless and finite: at most ten functions after initial observations, at most sixteen input rows. No PAC or novel-algorithm claim.
